Computational engineer with over seven years of experience in CFD, fluid–structure interaction and scientific software for wind energy, marine and aerospace applications: from numerical method to validated result, with GPU-accelerated solvers (Python, C++, CUDA), coupling with OpenFOAM and validation against experiments.

Experience

2023 – present

PhD candidate · computational aerodynamics

TU Delft, Wind Energy section

  • Designed and built OpenONDA, an open-source Python/C++ framework coupling a GPU-accelerated vortex-particle solver with OpenFOAM; accepted in the OpenFOAM Journal (2026).
  • Added large-eddy simulation to the particle solver and applied it to multirotor wind turbines and wind-farm momentum entrainment; published in Wind Energy Science (2025).
  • Co-supervised an MSc thesis (GPU fast multipole method); supervise BSc project teams.

2021 – 2022

Research engineer

RivalLab, Queen’s University · project with Pratt & Whitney Canada

I study unsteady flows, turbulence and fluid–structure interaction, combining numerical methods with experimental flow measurements. My work spans wind-energy wakes, marine dynamics and coherent structures in particle-tracking data. I develop hybrid Eulerian–Lagrangian methods and scientific software to connect physical models with measurements across scales.
