Wind energy · 2025

Wake recovery behind a multirotor with lifting wings

  • Wind energy
  • Aerodynamics
  • Flow control

Wings placed behind a multirotor shed tip vortices that lift low-momentum wake air and draw faster air down from above. Steady CFD, compared with wind-tunnel measurements, covers one-, two- and four-wing layouts; in the modelled cases the wake recovers faster with the wings than without. The OpenFOAM case represents rotor thrust and wing lift as momentum sources on selected cells.

Flow diagnostics · 2021

Vortex detection in sparse particle tracks by coherent-structure colouring

  • Fluid mechanics
  • Flow diagnostics

Coherent-structure colouring (CSC) groups particle tracks by how their mutual distances change in time. Applied to measured tracks in an Ahmed-body wake and a swirling jet, detection was accurate for mean inter-particle distances below about 15 % of the structure’s characteristic size, deteriorated quickly beyond that, and depended little on track length. The Python code includes the Ahmed-body tracks.

Flow diagnostics · 2021

Coherent sets from persistent Voronoi neighbours

  • Fluid mechanics
  • Dynamical systems
  • Flow diagnostics

The method records how long each pair of tracers remains Voronoi neighbours along their tracks and converts this into one spectral colour per tracer, without fixing the number of clusters in advance. It was tested on the double gyre and on sparse three-dimensional measurements behind a bluff body.

Fluid–structure interaction · 2019

Reynolds-number and damping effects on vortex-induced vibration

  • Fluid mechanics
  • Fluid–structure interaction

An elastically mounted cylinder free to move across the flow synchronises with its own vortex shedding over a range of flow speeds. Two- and three-dimensional CFD maps how Reynolds number and structural damping change the amplitude response branches and the wake modes. The repository generates an equivalent OpenFOAM case from one script.

Structural optimisation · 2019

Linear sizing of statically determinate trusses with self-weight

  • Structural mechanics
  • Optimisation

In a statically determinate truss, member forces stay linear in the member areas even with self-weight, so minimum-mass sizing under stress limits becomes a linear problem. The paper tests the method on benchmarks from 10 to 1,240 members. The Python re-implementation sizes the 37-member benchmark to 13.15 kg (paper: 13.23–13.35 kg).

Regular and chaotic orbits in the circular restricted three-body problem

  • Dynamical systems
  • Celestial mechanics

Lid-driven cavity flow

  • Fluid mechanics
  • Numerical methods

A compact incompressible Navier–Stokes solver for the two-dimensional lid-driven cavity: staggered grid, explicit time stepping and a pressure projection solved with a discrete cosine transform. The Re = 1000 solution is checked against the Ghia, Ghia & Shin (1982) benchmark.

Scientific software · Rotor aerodynamics

Lifting-line model of a wind-turbine rotor

  • Wind energy
  • Aerodynamics
  • Vortex methods

Each blade carries bound vortices whose trailing filaments follow a prescribed helical wake; the circulation is iterated against airfoil polars until the loads agree. The solver returns spanwise loads, induction factors, and thrust and power coefficients; the three-bladed example solves in under a second.

Scientific software · Unsteady aerodynamics

Vortex wakes of pitching and plunging airfoils

  • Aerodynamics
  • Vortex methods
  • Numerical methods

The airfoil is a row of vortex panels. At every time step the solver enforces zero normal flow and Kelvin’s circulation theorem, sheds a vortex from the trailing edge and lets the wake roll up freely. A thousand time steps take about two seconds.
